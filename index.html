About Abdulaziz

Hi, I'm Abdulaziz — a local guide from the Atlas Mountains with over 5 years of experience showing this region to visitors. Based in Marrakech, I run day trips into the mountains that pair the energy of the city with the landscapes I grew up in. As an Imazighen, I take pride in sharing my heritage: real hikes, real village life, real conversations — not a script. Let me show you the Morocco that locals actually know.
